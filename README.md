Pharmacy Management System 💊
A robust database project designed to revolutionize pharmacy operations. This system centralizes critical data—including medication inventory, customer information, prescriptions, and sales—to empower pharmacists, enhance patient safety, and streamline workflows.

🎯 Project Goals
The core mission of this project is to drive healthcare practices forward by promoting efficiency, accuracy, and patient-centricity. By providing a reliable and integrated system, we aim to:

Empower Pharmacists to make informed, data-driven decisions.

Enhance Patient Safety by automatically identifying potential drug interactions.

Optimize Workflows by streamlining the ordering and inventory management processes.

Strengthen Customer Relationships through accessible patient profiles and prescription histories.

✨ Key Features
Centralized Database: Manages medication inventory, customer profiles, orders, sales, and prescriptions in one place.

Inventory Management: Optimizes stock levels and streamlines the ordering process to ensure timely availability of medications.

Patient Safety Core: Includes a crucial module for checking and flagging potential drug interactions, minimizing adverse effects.

Informed Counseling: Equips pharmacists with accurate medication information and patient history for precise counseling.

Efficient & Accurate: Built to improve the overall efficiency and accuracy of pharmacy operations.
